# $100M Offers — 60-second Reel Scripts

Two reels from Alex Hormozi's *$100M Offers*, built as a series ("$100M Offers · Part 1/2").

---

## Reel 1: The Value Equation

**Concept:** People don't buy on price, they buy on value, and Hormozi gives a formula for value. The reel teaches that formula through two editors with the same skill charging very different prices.

**Pace:** about 160 words, spoken fast and with energy.

### 0–3s: Hook
- **VO:** "Same skill. Same software. One editor charges ₹500… the other charges ₹50,000."
- **On-screen:** Split screen. Left side **₹500 😐**, right side **₹50,000 😎**
- **Edit:** Start on a hard cut with no fade-in. Each price slams in with a "pop" sound.

### 3–8s: Setup
- **VO:** "The difference isn't talent. It's the *offer*. And Alex Hormozi gave us the formula."
- **On-screen:** The book cover (shoot your own copy in your hand) plus the text **"$100M Offers"**
- **Edit:** Punch in to 115% on "offer." A whoosh into the book shot.

### 8–12s: The formula
- **VO:** "Value equals dream outcome times likelihood… divided by time delay times effort."
- **On-screen:** The full equation builds on screen, term by term (After Effects, explained below)
- **Edit:** This is the frame people save. Hold it on screen for about 1 second.

### 12–22s: The top half
- **VO:** "Push the top UP. Dream outcome: don't sell 'editing,' sell 'reels that bring you clients.' Likelihood: show proof, results, and a guarantee."
- **On-screen:** Green **↑** arrows next to each term. "Editing" gets crossed out and replaced with "Reels that get clients"
- **Edit:** Add a strikethrough animation, plus a "ding" sound on the replacement text.

### 22–34s: The bottom half
- **VO:** "Push the bottom DOWN. Time: '3 reels in 48 hours.' Effort: 'Send raw footage. You touch nothing.'"
- **On-screen:** Red **↓** arrows. A mock WhatsApp chat showing "Raw footage sent ✅"
- **Edit:** A quick zoom-out on each down arrow. Make the chat mockup yourself; don't use a real client's chat.

### 34–48s: The insight
- **VO:** "Most people compete on price, and that's a race to the bottom. Hormozi says to stack so much value that the price looks small: bonuses, a guarantee, and a name that sells the result."
- **On-screen:** A price tag that shrinks as the words **Bonus + Guarantee + Name** stack above it
- **Edit:** This stretch is long, so cut to a new angle or B-roll every 2–3 seconds to keep attention.

### 48–60s: Payoff + loop
- **VO:** "So the ₹500 editor sells *editing*. The ₹50,000 editor sells *more clients, fast, zero effort, guaranteed.* Save this and rewrite your offer tonight… because right now, you're the ₹500 editor."
- **On-screen:** Return to the split screen from the hook. **"SAVE 📌"**
- **Edit:** The last line connects back to the hook, which pushes people to rewatch. Hard cut at the end with no outro.

### How to edit it (Premiere Pro + After Effects)

**1. Recording**
- Shoot vertical at 1080×1920, with the camera at eye level and a window to your side for light.
- Record each section 2–3 times, then keep the best take of each.
- Use a lav mic if you have one. If not, clean up the audio with **Essential Sound → Dialogue → Enhance Speech**.

**2. The rough cut**
- Create a 1080×1920, 30fps sequence.
- Go to **Text-Based Editing → Transcribe**, then delete the pauses, breaths, and "umm"s straight from the transcript.
- Target: no silence longer than 0.2s.
- Alternate between 100% and 115% scale on every second cut. This is the classic punch-in rhythm, and it hides jump cuts.

**3. Captions**
- Use **Captions → Create from transcript**, with a maximum of 2–4 words per line.
- Font: Montserrat ExtraBold or a similar bold sans, white with a soft black shadow.
- Highlight keywords (₹50,000, UP, DOWN, guarantee) in one brand color, such as yellow.
- **Safe zone:** keep text out of the bottom 20% and the right edge, where Instagram's buttons cover it.

**4. The equation (After Effects, the hero shot)**
- Build the fraction with a **Shape Layer** line animated using **Trim Paths** (0→100%).
- Type in each term with the **Typewriter** or **Fade Up by Character** preset.
- Make the top-half arrows green (↑) and the bottom-half arrows red (↓). Pop them in with scale overshoot (0→110→100%).
- Apply **Easy Ease (F9)**, then sharpen the curves in the Graph Editor for a snappy feel.
- Bring it into Premiere with **Dynamic Link** so you don't have to render it separately.

**5. Sound design**
- **Pop** on prices and keywords, **whoosh** on transitions, **riser** just before the formula reveal, **ding** on the "Save this" CTA.
- Music: use a track from Instagram's own library, or licensed stock. Don't use a trending song you don't have rights to.
- Set music around **-25 to -30 dB** under your voice, with **Essential Sound → Ducking** turned on.
- Peak voice levels around -6 dB. Overall loudness about **-14 LUFS**, using the **Loudness Meter** effect.

**6. Color**
- In Lumetri: add slight contrast, raise skin warmth by +5 to +10, and drop saturation a little on everything except your brand color.

**7. Export**
- **H.264**, 1080×1920, 30fps
- **VBR 1-pass, target 16 Mbps**
- **AAC 320 kbps, 48 kHz**
- Upload from your phone over Wi-Fi, and turn on "Upload at highest quality" in Instagram settings.

- **CTA in the caption:** "Comment OFFER and I'll rewrite yours"

## Reel 2: The Starving Crowd (general version)

**Concept:** Most people think a better product will fix their sales. Hormozi argues that the market you pick matters more than the product, the offer or your selling skill. This version works for anyone selling anything.

### 0–3s: Hook
- **VO:** "Your product isn't the problem. Your *customer* is."
- **On-screen:** **"PRODUCT ❌ CUSTOMER ✅"**
- **Edit:** Hard cut with a record-scratch sound on "customer."

### 3–10s: The ranking
- **VO:** "In *$100M Offers*, Hormozi ranks what decides whether you make money. Number 3: your sales skills. Number 2: your offer. Number 1… the market you pick."
- **On-screen:** A **#3 → #2 → #1** countdown
- **Edit:** Pause for half a second before "#1," with a riser underneath.

### 10–16s: The analogy
- **VO:** "He calls it the *starving crowd*. To sell food, you don't need the best recipe. You need a stadium full of hungry people."
- **On-screen:** Stadium crowd B-roll, with the text **"STARVING CROWD 🍔"**
- **Edit:** Add crowd-cheer audio under the B-roll and fade it out as you start talking again.

### 16–38s: The 4-point test
- **VO:** "Check 4 things. One: **massive pain**: they *need* it solved, not just want it. Two: **purchasing power**: they can actually pay. Three: **easy to target**: you know exactly where to find them. Four: **growing**: the market is getting bigger, not dying."
- **On-screen:** A 4-item checklist that builds line by line
- **Edit:** Cut to a new angle on each point, with a "tick" sound as each line appears.

### 38–50s: The scorecard
- **VO:** "'Fitness for everyone' fails. 'Fitness for busy IT professionals' passes all four. Back pain and weight gain, good salaries, all on LinkedIn, and the group keeps growing."
- **On-screen:** A two-column scorecard. **Everyone:** ❌❌❌❌ · **Busy IT pros:** ✅✅✅✅
- **Edit:** Make this the frame people screenshot. Hold it for a full second.

### 50–60s: CTA
- **VO:** "Before you fix your product, fix your crowd. Comment what you sell, and I'll tell you if your crowd is starving."
- **On-screen:** **"COMMENT WHAT YOU SELL 👇"**
- **Edit:** Punch in to 120% on "fix your crowd," then hard-cut out.

### Edit notes
Same base settings as Reel 1, plus:
- **Countdown (AE):** Animate each number's scale 300% → 100% with a slight motion blur. Add a quick camera shake (`wiggle(15,8)`) for 4 frames on impact.
- **Checklist (AE):** Draw each checkmark with **Trim Paths**. Stagger the lines 6 frames apart. Save it as a reusable template.
- **Scorecard:** Build it once in After Effects with the colors locked: red ❌ #FF3B30 and green ✅ #34C759.
- **Stadium B-roll:** Take it from Pexels or another licensed stock site. Add a slow push-in from 100% to 108%.
- **Hook visual:** Show a product on a shelf with nobody around it, then cut to a crowd.
- **Scorecard B-roll:** Stock footage of someone at a laptop rubbing their back to illustrate the IT pros example.

### Posting kit
- **Cover text:** "Why good products don't sell"
- **First line of the caption:** "Sales skill is #3. Here's #1 👇"

You can swap in a different example for the scorecard, e.g. coaching for first-time founders, or skincare for men in their 20s.
