## Fact check (checked 21 Nov 2026)

- **$1.5T is the fully diluted number.** Fully diluted (including options and restricted stock) the IPO valued Anthropic at about $1.5 trillion; non-diluted, about $1.1 trillion.
- **"Overnight" is the hook, not what happened.** The company is five years old; it was valued at $380B in February 2026.
- **The millionaires are on paper.** Sacra estimates roughly 2,800 current and former employees could hold at least $1M of pretax vested equity. What they actually get depends on vesting, taxes, share price and selling restrictions.
- **Deal size:** $81.3B raised in the IPO plus $26B in a private placement, about $107.3B total.
- **Pricing:** offered at $83; shares opened at $111.50.
- **CFO example:** stock options at a $1.30 exercise price vs the $83 IPO price (~64x, on paper).

Sources: Reuters, Bloomberg, NPR, Sacra.

## Reel script (45–55 sec)

**Hook (0–3s)**
> "Thousands of people became millionaires on the same day. They didn't win the lottery. They had a job."

**Setup (3–10s)**
> "Anthropic, the company behind Claude, just went public. It raised about 107 billion dollars. That's the biggest IPO in history."

**Simple explanation (10–18s)**
> "An IPO is like a private shop opening its doors. Before, only the founders, the staff and big investors could own a piece. Now anyone can buy one."

**The analogy (18–28s)**
> "Startups pay staff partly in salary and partly in pizza slices. Those slices are called equity. When the company grew to about 1.5 trillion dollars, every slice grew with it."

**Real example (28–36s)**
> "Their CFO got stock options at 1 dollar 30 each. The IPO price was 83 dollars. That's roughly 64 times the money, on paper."

**The twist (36–45s)**
> "But it wasn't overnight. It took five years. And most of this money is still on paper: taxes, vesting and lock-ups mean a lot of staff can't sell yet."

**Lesson + CTA (45–55s)**
> "Here's the lesson. Salary pays you for your hours. Equity pays you for growth. Complex in, clear out. Follow Decoded by Mayank for more."
