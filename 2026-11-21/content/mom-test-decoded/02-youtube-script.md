### 0:00 — Hook

\[Close-up. No intro music.\]

Maan lo tumne 6 mahine ek app pe lagaye. Launch kiya. Aur zero log aaye.

Par launch se pehle tumne 20 logon se poocha tha — "bhai idea kaisa hai?" Aur sabne bola: "Mast hai, main toh pakka use karunga."

Toh galti kahan hui?

\[Text: "They didn't lie. You asked wrong."\]

Galti unki nahi thi. Galti question ki thi. Aaj ek book decode karte hain — *The Mom Test* by Rob Fitzpatrick — jo sikhati hai ki logon se aise questions kaise poochho ki woh chaah ke bhi jhooth na bol paayein. Even your mom.

### 0:45 — Intro

\[Logo sting: Decoded by Mayank. "Complex in. Clear out."\]

Main hoon Mayank, aur is channel pe main business books ko simple language mein todta hoon, taaki tum unhe kal se use kar sako.

Is video ke end tak tumhare paas hoga: 3 rules, bad data pehchanne ka tareeka, aur ek ready-made question script jo tum client, customer ya startup idea — kisi pe bhi use kar sakte ho.

Rob khud Y Combinator se nikle founder hain. Facebook se pehle woh social advertising mein startup bana rahe the — toh galat feedback ki keemat unhone khud chukayi hai.

### 1:30 — The problem: everyone lies to be nice

\[B-roll: mom in kitchen, son showing phone.\]

Book ki shuruaat ek simple story se hoti hai. Ek ladka apni mom ko apna digital cookbook app idea dikhata hai. "Mom, kya aap aisa app use karogi?" Mom bolti hai: "Haan beta, bahut accha hai."

Ab socho — mom kya bolegi? Mom tumhara dil nahi todna chahti. Woh tumse pyaar karti hai. Toh woh jhooth bolti hai — bura jhooth nahi, pyaar wala jhooth.

Aur yeh sirf mom nahi karti. Dost karte hain, cousins karte hain, LinkedIn connections karte hain. Jab tum apna idea saamne rakhte ho, saamne wala tumhari feelings protect karta hai.

\[Text: "Your idea on the table = their honesty off the table."\]

Analogy: doctor ke waiting room mein jaake poocho "main beemar lag raha hoon?" — sab bolenge "nahi bhai, theek lag rahe ho." Asli doctor tumhari baat pe nahi, tumhare blood test pe chalta hai.

The Mom Test woh blood test hai. Hum yeh nahi poochte ki log kya *sochte* hain. Hum dekhte hain unhone kya *kiya*.

Usi story mein, second time ladka app ka naam bhi nahi leta. Woh poochta hai — "Mom, aap iPad pe kya kya karti ho? Aakhri baar nayi recipe kab try ki, kahan se mili?" Aur suddenly real facts aate hain, jo batate hain ki uska plan kahan kamzor hai aur asli customer kaun ho sakta hai. Mom jhooth bol hi nahi paayi, kyunki baat idea ki thi hi nahi.

### 3:00 — The 3 rules

\[Graphic: 3 cards appear one by one.\]

**Rule 1: Unki life ki baat karo, apne idea ki nahi.**

Jaise hi tum pitch karte ho, conversation tumhare baare mein ho jaata hai. Aur log tumhe khush rakhne lagte hain. Unki problems, unka din, unka kaam — bas wahi.

**Rule 2: Past ki specific baat poocho, future ki opinion nahi.**

"Kya aap kabhi aisa product kharidoge?" — yeh future hai. Har koi bolega haan, kyunki bolne mein kuch nahi lagta.

"Pichli baar is problem pe paisa kab kharch kiya?" — yeh past hai. Isme jhooth bolna mushkil hai, kyunki ya toh hua hai ya nahi hua.

\[Text: "Future = guess. Past = data."\]

**Rule 3: Kam bolo, zyada suno.**

Agar call mein tum zyada bol rahe ho, toh tum seekh nahi rahe — tum bech rahe ho. Silence se mat daro. Log khaali jagah bharne ke liye sach bol dete hain.

### 4:15 — Bad questions vs good questions

\[Split screen: red X left, green tick right.\]

Chalo kuch examples dekhte hain.

❌ "Kya yeh idea accha hai?" → ✅ "Abhi aap yeh problem kaise handle karte ho?"

❌ "Aap is pe kitna pay karoge?" → ✅ "Abhi is pe kitna kharch hota hai?"

❌ "Kya aap aisa app use karoge?" → ✅ "Aakhri baar yeh problem kab aayi? Step by step batao kya hua."

❌ "Aapko kya feature chahiye?" → ✅ "Ab tak kya kya try kiya? Kyun kaam nahi kiya?"

Aur chaar magic follow-ups jo har conversation mein daalne hain:

1. "Aap yeh karte hi kyun ho?" — motivation milta hai.
2. "Agar yeh solve na ho toh kya hoga?" — pata chalta hai problem badi hai ya bas chhoti chidchidahat.
3. "Pehle kya try kiya?" — agar kuch try nahi kiya, toh dard asli nahi hai.
4. "Aur kisse baat karun?" — ek conversation teen ban jaata hai.

### 5:30 — The 3 types of bad data

\[Graphic: three trash bins labelled.\]

Ab sabse important part. Conversation mein teen cheezein aayengi jo data jaisi lagti hain, par kachra hain.

**1. Compliments.** "Wow, bahut cool hai!" Rob ek line mein bolte hain: "Compliments are the fool's gold of customer learning." Chamakta hai, par sona nahi hai. Compliment mile toh khush mat ho — politely ignore karo aur wapas facts pe aao: "Thanks! Waise abhi aap yeh kaam kaise karte ho?"

**2. Fluff.** "Main usually…", "Main zaroor karunga…", "Haan, main dekh sakta hoon ki use karunga…" Yeh sab hawa hai. Fix simple hai — isko zameen pe le aao: "Aakhri baar kab kiya tha? Batao kaise."

**3. Ideas.** Log bolenge "bhai yeh feature daal do." Feature request ko samjho, follow mat karo. Poocho: "Woh feature aapko kya karne dega?" Neeche asli problem chhupi hoti hai — aur aksar uska solution simple hota hai.

\[Text: "Understand ideas. Don't obey them."\]

Ek aur cheez — approval maangna band karo. "Toh aapko kaisa laga?" yeh question compliment ke liye machhli pakadna hai. Jab tum ego dikhate ho, log tumhe bachane lagte hain.

### 7:15 — Ask the scary question

\[Moody lighting change.\]

Achha questions poochna kaafi nahi hai. *Important* questions poochne hain. Important question kaunsa? Jiska jawab tumhara poora business badal de — ya khatam kar de.

Har conversation mein ek question aisa hona chahiye jisse tumhe darr lage. Jaise: "Kya aap is pe abhi paisa kharch karte ho?" Kyunki agar jawab "nahi" hai — toh shayad business hi nahi hai.

Do thought experiments karo:

- "Agar yeh fail hua, toh sabse likely reason kya hoga?"
- "Yeh huge success bane, iske liye kya sach hona chahiye?"

Jo jawab aaye, wahi tumhare questions hain.

Aur yaad rakho — ek thanda sa "theek hai" mein ek excited "WOW" se zyada sach hota hai. Lukewarm reaction ko seriously lo. Bura news = progress. Galat hona frustrating hai, par wahi seekhna hai.

Ek trap bhi hai — "premature zooming." Seedha details mein mat ghuso. Pehle broad poocho: "Aapke kaam ki sabse badi problem kya hai?" Agar tumhari wali problem list mein aati hi nahi — toh signal mil gaya.

### 8:45 — Keep it casual

\[B-roll: chai tapri, two people chatting.\]

Log sochte hain customer research matlab formal meeting, calendar invite, "kya main aapka interview le sakta hoon?"

Rob bolte hain — skip the meeting. Jaise hi tum "interview" bolte ho, saamne wala formal ho jaata hai, aur formal log polite hote hain. Polite = bekaar data.

Isse ek casual chai pe baat jaisa rakho. Kisi event pe mile, kisi ke kaam ke baare mein curiosity se poocho. Shuruaati conversations 5–15 minute ki ho sakti hain. Agar sahi kiya, toh unhe pata bhi nahi chalega ki tum apne idea ke baare mein baat kar rahe the.

### 9:45 — Commitment: the real test

\[Graphic: three coins — Time, Reputation, Money.\]

Ab maan lo tumhara product thoda ready hai, aur tum use dikha rahe ho. Yahan ek naya trap hai.

Rob kehte hain koi meeting "achhi gayi" nahi hoti. Ya toh woh ek clear next step pe khatam hui — ya fail hui.

Agar har call "bahut badhiya, touch mein rehte hain" pe khatam hoti hai — congratulations, tumhara startup friend-zone ho gaya hai. \[Text: "Your startup got friend-zoned 💔"\] Inhe kehte hain zombie leads — zinda lagte hain, par kabhi convert nahi hote.

Asli interest teen currency mein dikhta hai:

1. **Time** — next meeting, trial use karna, feedback dena.
2. **Reputation** — apne boss ya dost se intro karwana, testimonial dena.
3. **Money** — pre-order, deposit, paid pilot.

Jitna zyada woh dete hain, utna zyada unke words pe bharosa karo.

Toh har conversation ke end mein ek concrete ask rakho. "Kya main aapke next project pe free trial kar sakta hoon?" "Kya aap mujhe aapke partner se milwa sakte ho?" Unhe "na" bolne ka asli mauka do. Jab tak woh mauka nahi diya, woh lead hai hi nahi.

Aur tumhare first customers thode "pagal" lagenge — product adhoora hoga, phir bhi woh excited honge, paisa dene ko ready honge. Inhe earlyvangelists kehte hain. Agar koi tumhari problem ke baare mein emotional nahi hai, woh first customer nahi banega.

### 11:30 — Finding people to talk to

\[Screen recording: DM inbox.\]

"Theek hai Mayank, par baat kisse karein?"

Cold outreach kaam karta hai — reply rate kam hota hai, par 2–3 conversations bhi bahut sikhaati hain. Par cold conversations ka asli goal hai… cold conversations band karna. Matlab, warm intros tak pahunchna.

Book ek formula deti hai message ke liye — 5 parts:

1. **Vision** — tum kya problem solve karna chahte ho.
2. **Framing** — tum kis stage pe ho. "Kuch bech nahi raha."
3. **Weakness** — tum kahan atke ho.
4. **Pedestal** — *woh hi* kyun help kar sakte hain.
5. **Ask** — chhota, specific request.

Log entrepreneurs ki help karna pasand karte hain — bas time waste nahi hona chahiye. Aur ek secret: log apne baare mein baat karna pasand karte hain… aur usse bhi zyada apni problems ke baare mein.

Best tareeka? Apne customers ki community mein ghus jao. Wahan sikhao, post karo, help karo. Phir log khud tumhare paas aayenge.

### 12:45 — Choose ONE customer

\[Graphic: big circle "Everyone" shrinking to a tiny dot.\]

Sabse common galti: "Mera product sabke liye hai."

Book mein ek founder hai jo ek powdered condiment bana rahi thi — bodybuilders se leke moms tak sabko bechna chahti thi. Result? Confusion, mixed feedback, zero progress. Jab usne sirf chhote bachon wali moms aur local health food stores pe focus kiya, tab cheezein chalne lagi.

Rule: pehle kisi ek ko serve karo, phir sabko. Ek accha customer segment ek "who-where" pair hota hai — kaun hai, aur woh kahan milega. Agar tum nahi bata sakte ki unhe kahan dhoondhoge, toh segment abhi bhi bahut bada hai. Aur kaato.

### 13:45 — How I'm using this

\[Talking head, personal tone.\]

Main khud apni agency Creation100x ke liye outreach kar raha hoon. Yeh book padhne ke baad main apne DMs badal raha hoon. Services ki list nahi — pehle questions — "aapke leads abhi kahan se aate hain? Aakhri reel kisne banayi thi? Usse kuch hua?" Aur har conversation ke end mein ek chhota sa ask.

Process simple rakho: call se pehle 3 questions likho. Call mein notes lo — exact words. Call ke baad 5 minute review karo: maine kya seekha, kya galat socha tha?

Toh recap:

- Har call ek commitment pe khatam karo.
- Ek customer chuno, sab nahi.

Is hafte ka challenge: 5 logon se baat karo. Ek baar bhi apna idea mat batao. Comment mein batao kya seekha.
