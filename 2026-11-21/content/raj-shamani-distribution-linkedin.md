# LinkedIn post — Raj Shamani's #1 podcast: distribution, strategy, execution

## The post

```
An Indian podcast just passed Joe Rogan on YouTube.

Raj Shamani's Figuring Out hit 22M subscribers.
That's more than The Diary of a CEO and The Joe Rogan Experience.
He added 8M of those in 2026 alone.

Most people will say "great guests" or "luck."

It's neither.
It's distribution + strategy + execution.

Here's what I learned breaking it down 👇

1/ He failed first.
His first podcast in 2017 got shut down because nobody knew it existed.
Lesson: a great show nobody sees is a dead show.

2/ He treated guests as distribution.
For the next show, he reached out to around 400 people.
Every big guest brings their own audience with them.

3/ One episode, three channels.
Main podcast. Clips channel. Shorts channel.
Shorts find new people.
Clips hook them.
The full episode keeps them.
That's one conversation in front of three different attention spans.

4/ He follows the platform. Blindly.
His rule: when a platform tells you what it's going to do next, just do that.
No ego. No "but my style is different."

5/ He played for scale, not RPM.
Most creators chase high-paying US views.
He called that a trap and built for reach first.
Now 22% of his subscribers are international anyway.

6/ He just kept shipping.
500+ episodes.
His book is literally called "Build, Don't Talk."

The lesson for anyone building a personal brand:

Content earns respect.
Distribution earns reach.
Execution earns #1.

Most of us spend 90% of our time on the content and 10% on getting it seen.
He flipped that.

Which one are you weakest at: content, distribution, or execution? 👇

♻️ Repost this for someone who's making great content nobody sees.

#RajShamani #Podcasting #ContentStrategy #PersonalBranding #YouTube
```
